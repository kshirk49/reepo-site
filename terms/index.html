Beta tester agreement

By ticking the box in Reepo, you agree to the following with Kyle Shirk and Michael Harrison, who make Reepo (“we”). Reepo isn’t a company yet; once it is, it takes our place in this agreement and we’ll tell you.

  1. It’s a test version. Reepo is pre-release software, given as is, with no warranty. It may have bugs, and it may lose data. Keep your own backups of your projects and of ~/Reepo (Time Machine is enough). To the extent the law allows, our liability is limited to what you paid, which is nothing.
  2. Your projects stay untouched. Reepo makes its stems from copies and never writes to your project files. If you ever find a project file changed, stop and tell us at once.
  3. Your music stays yours. You give us only the permission needed to store your files, process them (previews, tags, sound fingerprints for search) and show them to the people you share them with. We don’t train anything on your audio. The tagger learns from your tag picks only if you choose “Help train the tagger”, and even then it gets your tags and stem names, never audio.
  4. The cloud is optional. Until we tell you the outside security review is done, put only music in the cloud that you could afford to have leak. Anything you don’t sign in for stays on your Mac.
  5. You have the rights to everything you put in Reepo’s cloud or share through it.
  6. Keep it between us. Don’t share the app, screenshots, its features or how it performs in public until we say so. This ends when Reepo launches publicly, or 12 months after you agree, whichever comes first.
  7. Feedback. We may use any feedback you give, forever, without paying for it. We don’t have to use it.
  8. Usage and crash reports are on unless you turn them off in Settings → Help. They never carry your audio or the names of your projects, tracks or files (see the privacy notice).
  9. The beta cloud is temporary. We may reset it with 7 days’ notice. We delete beta cloud data 30 days after the beta ends, unless it moves into a real account.
  10. Either side can end this at any time. There’s nothing to buy and no fee. Ask and we delete your account and your cloud data.
  11. Reepo stays ours. We let you use Reepo for testing only. Don’t copy it, pass it on, take it apart to see how it works, or use what you learn in the beta to build or help build a competing product. Reepo, its code and its design belong to us.

Questions: hello@reepo.studio.
