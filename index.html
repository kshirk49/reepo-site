Reepo has your DAW render every track from a copy of your project, with wet, dry and True versions of each. They’re your real tracks, not a guess pulled out of the mix.

Tagged.

Reepo labels each stem by instrument and works out the song’s key and tempo. Later, you can find a stem by typing what it sounds like.

True stems.

When you bounce stems one at a time, the compressor and limiter on your master never hear the whole mix, so the stems don’t add up to your song. True stems add up to it exactly. If a mix can’t be matched exactly, Reepo tells you.

Kick + Bass + Vox + Keys + Guitar + Pad= your master.
Nothing missing.

Does it touch my project?

Never.

Reepo works on a copy. Your original isn’t opened, saved over or moved.

Can I use my Mac meanwhile?

Yes.

Reepo runs your DAW in the background, so you can keep working in other apps.
